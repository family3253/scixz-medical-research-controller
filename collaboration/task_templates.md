# Collaboration task templates

## Manuscript review

Mode: `council`  
Roles: methodology, statistics, clinical, reviewer, editor, critic, consensus, verifier.  
Primary question: Can the manuscript's design, analysis, and claims support publication at the target venue?  
Required outputs: major/minor concerns, evidence anchors, priority, uncertainty, and the formal output contract of the selected review Skill.

## Target-trial or RWE design

Mode: `council`  
Roles: clinical, methodology, statistics, literature, critic, consensus, verifier.  
Required checks: target population, treatment strategies, comparator, time zero, eligibility, estimand, outcome, censoring, confounding, missingness, sensitivity analyses, and design limitations.

## GEO, bulk RNA-seq, or scRNA-seq

Mode: `panel` or `council` when biological interpretation is consequential.  
Roles: bioinformatics, statistics, methodology, clinical, pathway/visualization, critic, verifier.  
Required checks: dataset identity, biological replication, QC, batch, statistical unit, multiple testing, validation, and association-versus-mechanism language.

## Journal selection

Mode: `panel`  
Roles: journal, editor, methodology, literature, verifier.  
Required outputs: scope fit, article type, evidence of recent comparable papers, current policy/metric verification, desk-reject risks, and fallback cascade.
