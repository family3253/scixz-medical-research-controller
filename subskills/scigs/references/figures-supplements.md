# Figures, Tables, and Supplements

Use `asset_inventory.py` to record embedded images, explicit figure files, supplements, SHA-256 values, and simple in-text figure/table references. Then compare the inventory with the official journal requirements.

Check accepted formats, resolution/dimensions, color mode, file-size limits, naming, panel labels, caption placement, editable tables, legends, supplement citations, and anonymization. Never upscale a low-resolution image and claim that its source resolution requirement is satisfied.
