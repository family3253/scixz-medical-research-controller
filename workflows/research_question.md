# Research-question workflow

## Entry and scope

Use for research ideas, gaps, hypotheses, PICO/PECO, mechanism questions, and feasibility decisions. It does not claim novelty without a defined search boundary.

## Inputs

Require broad interest, population/condition, exposure/intervention, comparator, outcome, time horizon, available data, and the decision the study should inform. Missing the intended decision is blocking.

## Route

Controller → 中书省 frames the question → 门下省 challenges novelty/feasibility assumptions → `panel` with research, clinical, and methodology roles. Primary: `clinical-research-idea` or `research-ideation`. Supporting: `research-lit`, `novelty-check`, `design-study`, `experiment-plan`.

## Outputs

One primary question, secondary questions, hypotheses, PICO/PECO, novelty rationale, feasibility constraints, design ceiling, kill criterion, and next-step experiment/search plan.

## Verification

Check that the population, exposure, comparator, outcome, time, and mechanism are internally consistent; distinguish a research gap from a broad topic; label literature claims with sources or as unverified.

## Failure/fallback

If the topic is too broad, return a narrowed set of options and one focused question. If novelty cannot be checked, report the search limitation rather than claiming novelty.
