# 英文投稿综述写作规范

## 风格要求
- prefer analytical paragraphs over source-by-source listing
- foreground contribution, comparison, limitation, and synthesis
- keep claims proportional to evidence strength

## structure suggestions
1. introduction
2. conceptual or methodological foundations
3. major streams or application domains
4. comparative synthesis of evidence
5. open problems and limitations
6. future directions
7. conclusion

## required moves
- explain why studies differ
- identify evidence gaps
- avoid overclaiming from single studies
