# Study Type Router

Use this table to select a primary design, reporting guideline, and separate bias or quality tool. Verify current versions from official sources before finalizing.

| Primary aim | Typical design | Question framework | Reporting guidance | Bias/quality assessment | Registration or protocol note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intervention efficacy or effectiveness | Randomized trial, pragmatic trial, cluster trial | PICO(T) | CONSORT 2025 plus relevant extension; SPIRIT 2025 for protocols | RoB 2 | Prospectively register before first participant consent when required; prespecify outcomes and SAP |
| Non-randomized intervention effect | Cohort, quasi-experimental, target-trial emulation | PECO(T) | STROBE; RECORD for routinely collected data | ROBINS-I when applicable | Prespecify estimand, time zero, eligibility, treatment strategies, follow-up, and analysis |
| Etiology, association, or natural history | Cohort, case-control, cross-sectional | PECO(T) | STROBE; RECORD for EHR/claims | Select a domain-based tool appropriate to the design and inference | Avoid causal claims unless assumptions and design support them |
| Diagnostic accuracy | Prospective or retrospective diagnostic study | PIRD | STARD and relevant extensions | QUADAS-2 or current successor | Prespecify index/reference tests, thresholds, flow, timing, and indeterminate results |
| Prognostic factor | Cohort | PICOTS or PECO(T) | REMARK for tumor markers when relevant; applicable prognostic guidance | QUIPS when applicable | Define time zero, candidate factors, adjustment set, outcomes, and censoring |
| Clinical prediction model | Development, external evaluation, updating, or impact study | PICOTS | TRIPOD+AI | PROBAST+AI | State intended use; separate development, evaluation, and impact questions |
| Systematic review or meta-analysis | Evidence synthesis | PICO/PECO/PIRD/PICOTS | PRISMA 2020; PRISMA-P for protocols; extensions as applicable | RoB 2, ROBINS-I, QUADAS-2, PROBAST+AI, or other design-matched tools; AMSTAR 2 for review appraisal | Check PROSPERO or another appropriate registry before claiming novelty |
| Case report or case series | Descriptive report | Patient/problem-intervention-outcome where useful | CARE | Clinical plausibility and source verification; no causal inference | Obtain appropriate consent and institutional review |
| Qualitative study | Interviews, focus groups, ethnography | SPIDER | COREQ or SRQR | Use design-appropriate reflexivity and trustworthiness criteria | Plan sampling, saturation logic, reflexivity, and data governance |
| Quality improvement | Local intervention and iterative evaluation | PICO plus context | SQUIRE | Address secular trends, co-interventions, and generalizability | Distinguish QI from research under local policy |
| Economic evaluation | Trial- or model-based evaluation | PICO plus perspective and horizon | CHEERS | Validate model structure, inputs, uncertainty, and applicability | Prespecify perspective, horizon, discounting, and sensitivity analysis |
| Clinical practice guideline | Evidence-to-recommendation process | Structured clinical questions | RIGHT | AGREE II | Manage conflicts, stakeholder participation, updating, and certainty of evidence |

## Routing Rules

1. Choose the design from the clinical question, not from the available statistical package.
2. Separate multiple primary aims into linked studies or explicitly prioritized aims.
3. Treat reporting guidelines as completeness aids, not proof of low bias.
4. Add specialty or intervention extensions only after selecting the main guideline.
5. For clinical AI used as an intervention, add SPIRIT-AI, CONSORT-AI, DECIDE-AI, or other stage-appropriate guidance; for prediction reporting, use TRIPOD+AI.
6. For routinely collected health data, check RECORD in addition to STROBE.
7. If no listed tool fits, search the EQUATOR Network and the relevant methods organization rather than guessing.
