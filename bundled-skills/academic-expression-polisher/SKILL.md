# Academic Expression Polisher

## Purpose

Convert loose, over-decorated, or AI-sounding academic prose into precise, objective, publication-credible language.

## When to Use

Use this skill whenever the user:
- wants to reduce AI tone or machine-like phrasing in academic writing
- needs a paragraph made more objective, concise, or discipline-appropriate
- wants vague expressions replaced with more precise academic wording
- asks to remove emotional or promotional phrasing from scholarly text
- wants a revision table showing what was changed and why

## Core Rule

Do not increase density by making the text obscure. Precision is more important than ornament.

## Default Deliverable

Unless the user asks otherwise, provide:
1. a polished rewritten version
2. a compact change log or comparison table
3. a short note on the main language problems removed

## Workflow

1. Read the text closely.
2. Flag vague, emotional, inflated, or AI-patterned phrasing.
3. Replace them with more exact academic wording.
4. Return the revised text plus a concise explanation of major substitutions.

## Response Style

Be strict, clean, and neutral. The result should sound publishable rather than embellished.
