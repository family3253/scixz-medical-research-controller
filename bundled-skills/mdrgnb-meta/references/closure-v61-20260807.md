## STU-041 regression case

Report: Maestro-de la Calle et al., 2026, DOI `10.1093/jacamr/dlag140`.

- Exclude the confirmed-GNBSI derivation branch because current GNB infection is already known.
- Include only the febrile-neutropenia branch: at FN onset, before culture results, the model
  estimates concurrent CR-GNBSI in an organism-unknown clinical population.
- The four-component rule is a fixed expert/heuristic rule, not a fitted multivariable regression.
- The evaluation sampled 15 cases and 45 controls. Fifteen of 16 derivation cases overlap the
  evaluation; classify it as non-independent/unclear validation with high risk of bias.
- Reported sensitivity 0.867 and specificity 0.533 imply TP=13, FN=2, TN=24, FP=21.
- Reported PPV 2.75% and NPV 99.6% were reweighted to source prevalence 15/1001. Do not use them
  as raw sampled predictive values and do not reconstruct a second 2x2 table.
- No eligible-branch AUC, quantitative calibration, or net-benefit analysis was reported.

This case proves that report-level inclusion does not make every branch eligible. Apply eligibility
and synthesis membership at report × cohort × outcome × model × dataset × analysis-population level.
